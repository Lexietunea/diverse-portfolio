About

I’m a 23-year-old developer in Nigeria who takes a product from contract to interface on my own. I move between ecosystems easily: an AMM on Arc one week, private lending on Solana the next, then a live fan platform on Base. I write the contracts, the backend and the front end, and I deploy it.

When something in my own life is annoying, I engineer a fix with the same care I give client-grade work. I like to build something complete first, then cut it down until only what matters is left.
